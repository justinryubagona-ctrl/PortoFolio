Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know
about working together.

When you hire an agency, your project is typically pitched by senior directors but handed off to junior designers. Working with me means 100% of your product design, architecture, and interaction craft is executed personally by a principal designer with 12+ years of high-growth experience. Faster execution, zero bureaucracy, and surgical precision.
Standard SaaS product design engagements generally run between 4 to 8 weeks depending on scope, state complexity, and deliverables. For fast-moving startups looking to launch an MVP or redesign a core funnel, accelerated 3-week design sprints are also available.
While my primary discipline is product design and design systems, I build production-ready front-end code prototypes (React, HTML5, CSS3, WebGL, TypeScript). I also conduct hands-on paired engineering handoffs and design QA to ensure the shipped product matches the Figma specification down to the exact sub-pixel.
I regularly work with stealth venture-backed startups and publicly traded corporations. I am more than happy to sign a mutual NDA prior to our first deep-dive discovery call, and all project files and intellectual property remain 100% your proprietary assets.
